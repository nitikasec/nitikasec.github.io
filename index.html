About

What I do and what I'm known for.

I'm an offensive security analyst with 1+ year of professional experience and 100+ assessments across web, API, mobile, and cloud. Over the past year I've specialized in AI-agent and LLM security — how AI systems that use tools and read untrusted content can be attacked, and how to actually defend them. My core thesis: a model refusing is not a security control — enforcement has to live below the model, in the tool layer.
